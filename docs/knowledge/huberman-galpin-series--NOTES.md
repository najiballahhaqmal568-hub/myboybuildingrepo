## Master synthesis

**Galpin's laws**: methods are many, concepts are few · consistency beats intensity · there are no free passes in physiology · stress+recovery=adaptation · you're an athlete if you have a body.

**The unified system across all six episodes**:
1. **Assess** (EP1): annual battery, train your worst pillar most often.
2. **Program** (EP4): SMART goal minus 10% → life quadrant → calendar-first → complexity ladder before load → priority first → +3%/wk intensity, ≤10%/wk volume → deload every 4–8 wks → pre-mortem chaos management.
3. **Strength** (EP2): 3-to-5 everything, ≥70% 1RM, patterns incl. rotation.
4. **Hypertrophy** (EP2): 15–20 sets/muscle/wk @ 8–15 reps @ RIR 2, volume is king.
5. **Endurance** (EP3): ~2 hrs/wk covers all four types; ~5–6 min all-out intervals + one max-aerobic effort + long steady state.
6. **Recover** (EP5): down-regulation breathing after sessions; monitor triad ≥5 days before acting; heat > cold on growth days.
7. **Fuel** (EP6): protein 1 g/lb, hydration trio (0.5 oz/lb baseline → BW÷30 oz intra → 125% losses post), creatine forever, fish oil 2–4 g, single-ingredient supplements only.
