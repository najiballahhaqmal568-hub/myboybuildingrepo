## A. Training principles & programming

**Seven variables of any program [39]**: Choice, Order, Volume, Intensity, Frequency, Rest, Progression.
- Hypertrophy: order barely matters · protect weekly volume · rest 30s–5min all fine · don't hammer one muscle back-to-back days.
- Strength/power: ORDER is critical (hardest first) · intensity (%1RM) is king · volume capped by force recovery · same muscles can train daily (little local fatigue) · ≥3 min rests.

**Goal-specific numbers [40]**: hypertrophy 8–12 typical (~70–80% 1RM), anything 5–30+ at equal effort · strength 3–5 reps maximal · power ≤6 reps fast (load varies: small lifts 30–50% 1RM, squat 50–60%, Olympic 80–95%) · strength/power rest ≥3 min — beginners take MORE rest than feels necessary (you can't perceive the last ~8% recovery).

**Minimal viable growth [42]**: slight surplus · protein floor 1.6 g/kg (2.2 fine, adds little) · full ROM · 15–20 working sets/muscle/week · 2–3×/muscle/week · **"72-hour rule": stimulate each muscle to a good pump every ~72h without annihilating it**.

**Abs are just muscle [01][03][08][09]**: ~50/50 fiber split like quads — no daily-training exemption. Core week = 4 categories (flexion / rotation / anti-extension / anti-rotation), one each minimum. Progression ladder: isometric holds 45–90s → anti-movement high-rep → loaded eccentric → heavy concentric 5–8 reps ("curl one vertebra at a time"). Abs blueprint: 2–4×/week, 10–20 sets/muscle/week, 0–2 RIR — thickness gains 10–20% in trials; fix anterior pelvic tilt for instant leanness.

**Glutes (Contreras) [22][23]**: #1 mistake = insufficient effort, then masking with variety/volume. Log every lift and chase it. Glute template: 3×/week, each session 1 exercise from each family — thrust/bridge · squat/lunge · hinge · abduction ("rule of thirds" rotating CNS-heavy vertical moves, low-damage horizontal moves, penalty-free abduction). Deep > shallow squats for glute/adductor growth.

**Strength standards & testing [41]**: bench ≈1× BW men (0.6 women) · leg press ≈2× BW (1.5) · grip >45 kg men / >28 kg women; side asymmetry >10% = 2.67× sarcopenia risk. Grip strength predicts lifespan better than size — $30 dynamometer = annual health KPI.

## B. Recovery: sleep, stress, breath

**Sleep is the highest-leverage free drug [30][38][16]**: target 7.5–8.5h. Landmark trial — identical deficit, 5.5h vs 8.5h sleep → **55% less fat lost, ~60% less lean retained**. Chronic <5h/night = +251% type-2-diabetes hazard. After a bad night: double/triple protein, keep walking, nap 30–90min. Tips: caffeine cutoff ≥8h pre-bed · no novel/agitating content at night · remove clocks/sleep scores · bedroom CO₂ <900ppm, noise <35dB · snoring → mouth tape + nasal dilator or sleep study. Men vs women sleep gap is real but ~25–30 min — don't engineer anxiety over viral claims.

**Stress down-regulation — the 5 Ps [07]**: Perspective (mantra) · Place (quiet/dark) · Position (pelvis above heart) · Pace of breath (slow deep ~5 min/day sweet spot, extend exhales) · Palpation (soft-ball self-massage). Stack all five in one 5-minute session.

**Breathing mechanics [06][45]**: Zone 1 belly (relaxing) · Zone 2 ribs (everyday ideal) · Zone 3 face/neck gasping (emergency only — chronic = jaw pain, headaches). Rib-release ball drill 2 min/side. Respiratory rate tracks CO₂/pH, not oxygen — **each +1 breath/min = ×1.25 stress odds, independent of HRV**: log it as an overload early-warning.

**HRV composure training [12]**: ~10s breath cycle (4s in, 6s out) builds "coherence"; find your personal resonance with feedback; when spiked mid-event, change ONLY the breath.

## C. Monitoring & overtraining

**Personal baseline dashboard [35]**: track 1 objective marker (RHR/HRV/bodyweight) + 1 subjective (mood/desire) + 1 performance anchor, ~30 days untouched. Flag: >1 SD from mean for 3–5 days = attention; >2 SD for 2 days = act now. Ignore single bad days.
**Blood work as business KPIs [32]**: not required, but establishes your "passport" baselines early 20s; interpret against YOUR history not lab ranges ($500–700/panel).
**Fitness dashboard [44]**: resting HR 40s–50s bpm good (>60 warrants attention) · VO2 max: average 35–45 ml/kg/min, independence threshold ~18, elite 50+ — two free yearly numbers.

## D. Nutrition & supplement stack (evidence-ranked)

| Item | Protocol | Payoff |
|---|---|---|
| **Fish oil** [34][29] | 2–4 g EPA+DHA/day (brain ceiling ~2g); omega-3 index target 8–10% | **+14–15% RMR, +4% lean mass over 12 wks**, brain perfusion, inflammation |
| **Creatine** [25][36] | 5–10 g/day maintenance; 20–30 g/day for 7 days in high-risk windows | Brain ATP buffer — cognition under sleep deprivation; TBI protection evidence ~2/5 |
| **Magnesium** [28] | 400 mg/day (bisglycinate/malate/threonate) | 600+ reactions; drops after TBI; cheapest multiplier |
| Collagen [20] | 5 g/day × 12 months | ~4–7% BMD gain at spine/femoral neck |
| Protein pre-bed [05] | ~40 g, <220 kcal, ~30 min before sleep | Raises overnight MPS; doesn't blunt fat loss; 12-wk: higher CSA + 1RM |
| Water [29] | 0.5 L pre-meal | RMR bump + satiety-driven weight loss |

Myth-busts: calcium pills above dietary intake add nothing; spicy food ≈ 3–5 kcal; green-tea/thermo stacks transient (+7–15% RMR for hours).

## E. Metabolism & body composition

**TDEE decomposition [31]**: EAT ~10–20% (hard to exceed 30%) · NEAT 5–30% (often rivals exercise!) · TEF 8–15% · RMR 40–80%. Higher TDEE = eat more while leaning out — build NEAT habits + muscle.
**Sleep × composition bidirectional [30]**: more muscle improves sleep; bad sleep wrecks both.

## F. Brain, bones, longevity

**Exercise program for the brain [27]**: resistance 2×/wk (5–8 whole-body exercises, ~2 sets each) + Norwegian 4×4 intervals 1–2×/wk + easy cardio 1–3×/wk. Grip strength explains ~30% of Alzheimer's death risk variance; even 1 lift session/wk improves cognition.
**VO2 max = compounding asset [43][46]**: mix ~70% low-intensity + real HIIT (30/30 ×4–12 or 4-min all-out ×2–4, truly maximal) 2–3×/wk. Mortality falls 64→18.6 per 10k men from least→most fit quintile; benefit never plateaus. Escaping the bottom fitness quintile is the biggest single win.
**Bones respond to CHANGE in load [19][21]**: LIFTMOR trial — 2×/wk, 30 min, deadlift+squat+OHP 5×5 @85% 1RM + jump-chin-drop: zero fractures, superior BMD in fragile postmenopausal women. Active kids carry ~10% more BMD into their 30s. Lifting never stunted anyone's growth (1960s myth from child labor confounds).
**Brain fuel rules [24][26]**: homocysteine <11, omega-3 index ≥8%, vitamin D/iron/B12 checked; post-concussion brain goes glucose-resistant → ketones/lactate enter freely (Galpin's ketone-ester call after head trauma).

## G. Mind & discipline

- **Mental toughness [15]**: "try harder" is the only variable that always transfers; willingness under discomfort is built purely by repeated exposure.
- **Self-talk tool [10]**: address yourself in second person through an admired mentor's voice (Ethan Kross, *Chatter*).
- **Visualization [11]**: most research-backed mental skill — match mode to purpose (confidence/coping/familiarization/technique); ~10 min 1–2×/day, always include emotion; NOT right before bed.
- **Suffering as dopamine repair [14]**: comfort-seeking hollows reward sensitivity; disciplined struggle restores it.
- **Timing [37]**: perform hard efforts at your habitual circadian time — timing alone predicted ~3% of Olympic swim results.
